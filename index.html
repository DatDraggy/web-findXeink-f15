Connection

Panel

The display broadcasts its own size and colour depth. This is read automatically — you should not have to know it.

If it will not appear

  1. Turn Location Services on. Android returns zero Bluetooth results with location off, silently. This is the most common cause by far.
  2. Un-pair the display in Android Settings if you ever paired it — a bonded device often stops advertising.
  3. Close the official app. The display accepts one connection at a time.
  4. Press its button to wake it immediately before searching.

Transfer

The destination screen is chosen per picture in Studio — the main screen uses opcodes A1/A2, the contact screen C1/C2.

A 200-byte chunk needs a negotiated MTU of at least 213. The browser will not tell us what it got, so if a send is never acknowledged the app retries automatically at 10 — exactly what the vendor app does.

About

An independent, unofficial app for FindXeink e-ink displays, built by reverse-engineering the vendor's Android app for interoperability. Not affiliated with or endorsed by FindXeink or Lenze Technology.
